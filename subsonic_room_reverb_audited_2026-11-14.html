Mapping: for the first axial mode, f₁ = c / (2L), therefore L = c / (2f₁). The beat detector searches pairs of chord partials and reports the smallest non-zero difference below 20 Hz: fᵦ = |n fᵢ − m fⱼ|. A subharmonic target is the root frequency divided by a selectable power of two.

Model boundary: the delay network uses this frequency to shape a feedback-delay reverb; the WebGL view illustrates the first axial standing-wave pattern. This is not a full finite-element room-acoustics solver: a real room's modes also depend on all three dimensions, boundary impedance, air absorption, and wall construction.
